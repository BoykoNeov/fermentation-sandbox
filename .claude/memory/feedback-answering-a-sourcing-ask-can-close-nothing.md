D-245 left "one sourcing ask gates four of the five remaining xfails". The ask was answerable in
full from primary sources -- and answering it closed **none** of the four. Propanol recovered 86 %
of its gap and still missed; both Minebois legs got **worse** on her own must (~1.7x her
measurement, where the "unfairly rich" fixture read ~1.1x). What the sourcing actually bought was
an unrelated, larger finding the supplement happened to carry: the model leaves 40.8 % of the
must's assimilable nitrogen standing where the paper measures 0.2 %.

**Why:** "we cannot settle this without the paper's medium" is a true statement about *evidence*
that reads as a claim about *cause*. Once written it becomes the whole explanation, and the beat
that finally gets the source arrives expecting a confirmation rather than a measurement.

**How to apply:** preregister before running -- which guards should move, in which direction, and
by roughly how much ([[feedback-verify-an-xfail-fails-for-its-stated-reason]] is the sibling).
Then **anchor the new harness**: if you reimplement a helper to score a different fixture,
reproduce the published numbers on the OLD fixture first, or the columns cannot be read against
each other. And when the ask is answered, rewrite the blocker strings immediately -- a reason
string still saying "needs the paper's medium" after the medium is in hand is the stale-breadcrumb
class this archive keeps paying for [[feedback-check-the-blocker-is-still-blocking]].
