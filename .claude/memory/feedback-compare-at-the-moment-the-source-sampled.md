The number D-251 priced was the model's peak cell nitrogen. Located, it falls at 20.5 % of
the nitrogen consumed; the source's first sample is at 50 %. So the peak was compared against no
measurement at all. Read at HER landmark the model carried 0.092 where her numbers imply
0.108-0.156 - the opposite sign to the one the peak suggested.

**Why:** a max over a trajectory is always available and never asks where the data are. It reads
like a worst case, which is why it survives review, but a worst case at an unobserved moment
settles nothing about agreement with a paper.

**How to apply:** take the source's sampling times first and read the model at those indices. If you
also report an extremum, report WHERE it falls in the source's own coordinates ("at 20.5 % of the
nitrogen gone, before her first sample at 50 %") and pin that location, so a future beat is told
when the peak moves onto a moment that IS measured.
