D-257 built the blended fusel rate law and reverted it because the shipped precursor sink rides
the alcohol rate (phenylalanine stranded, 72.9 %). D-259/D-260 measured the growth-anchored sink
that removes exactly that coupling and refused it because, under the SHIPPED rate law, Crépin's
split and Rollero's tracer trade one-for-one. Each refusal's ground was the other repair's defect,
three records apart, and both were quoted as settled. **Nobody had run the pair.**

**Why it matters:** a refusal is a statement about a configuration, not about a mechanism. Two
single-mechanism refusals do not bound their combination, and the archive's own prohibitions
("build stays owner-gated") were carrying an untested claim as though it had been measured.

**How to apply:** before quoting a refusal, list what else was live in the configuration it was
measured in. If a later beat has changed one of those things — or refused a repair for it — the
refusal is a candidate for re-measurement, and the cheapest instrument is the JOINT counterfactual
built from the two test-side pieces that already exist. Pre-register the joint prediction: at D-266
the pre-registered mechanism ("pays on the tracer") was WRONG, the joint arm sat inside Rollero's
band, and what it exposed instead was a different axis (isoleucine/valine/threonine over-shoot,
the propanol floor the growth sink alone breaks). A pair can fail on an axis neither half was
scored on — score the pair on everything, not on the two grounds that refused the halves.
