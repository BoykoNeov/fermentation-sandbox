The shipped uptake capacity crossed Crepin's landmark at r ~ 3.9, INSIDE its declared [0.5, 10]
band: no new parameter, no widened band, and the full suite cost only three re-pins. It was still
not taken. Front-loading is paid for in stored nitrogen, and landing her landmarks loads the cells
to 0.137-0.139 g N per g dry weight against the repo's own N-replete elemental reference of 0.114
(band top 0.14) — it spends essentially the whole band of a DIFFERENT parameter.

**Why:** a parameter's own uncertainty band is a statement about that parameter alone. Moving it
can spend a budget declared somewhere else — a mass, a charge, a per-cell content — and nothing in
the band checks that. The test suite passing is not the same as the physics being affordable.

**How to apply:** when a knob reaches a target, ask what physical quantity accumulates to pay for
it, express it in the units the literature states that quantity in, and compare against a SOURCED
ceiling before proposing the move. Report the price with the fit, in the same table.
