When you need "paper B uses the same defined medium as paper A", a shared **citation is not
evidence** — and neither is a shared lab lineage. Settle it on **composition**: reduce both to one
normalised frame (a %-of-total partition works even when the two are at different absolute levels)
and compare **species by species**, then state the agreement in the units of the *coarser* table's
printed resolution.

**Why:** at D-255 Rollero, Crépin and Minebois all cite Bely 1990, and D-254 had explicitly
forbidden inferring the medium from that. What settled it was his supplemental %YAN table against
Minebois's printed stock: **≤0.05 pp on all 19 species — exactly half his last printed digit.**
A citation could not have carried that; the table did, and it took one fetch.

**How to apply:**
- Prefer a table the paper wrote about its *own* medium over any recipe you reconstruct
  ([[feedback-a-generic-partition-is-not-a-defined-medium]], [[feedback-transcribe-tables-not-prose]]).
- **Quote agreement in ABSOLUTE units and check the RELATIVE view separately.** ≤0.05 pp looks
  uniform; on a 0.3 % species that is ±16.7 % relative, so per-species claims below ~1 % of the
  total are not supported. The resolution, not the agreement, bounds what you may build.
- **Cross-check against a table you did not read for the purpose.** Rollero's valine share
  predicted 65.0/232.0/394.4 µM initial against consumed valine of 62/221/376 already transcribed
  in the repo for another derivation — one consumption fraction (95.3 %) at all three levels.
  A transcription error or a wrong N-frame would not reproduce a constant.
- Watch for a row that is **not** the medium: the same table's "Valine Total" was *proteinogenic*
  valine and would have put the must 4× out ([[feedback-derive-the-papers-nitrogen-frame]]).
- Access: PMC's `/bin/` supplement URL sits behind a proof-of-work challenge and returns an HTML
  stub; EuropePMC's `webservices/rest/<PMCID>/supplementaryFiles` serves the same deposit
  unchallenged. Same deposit reached twice is **one source**, not two
  ([[feedback-paywalled-is-one-host]]).
