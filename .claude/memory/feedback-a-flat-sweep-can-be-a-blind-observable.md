D-249 swept the uptake capacity across 1000x and the biomass-versus-nitrogen shares moved in the
THIRD DECIMAL (0.543 -> 0.542), which read as "the knob cannot reach this, the rate law's form is
wrong". Scored in the frame the paper is written in, the same 1000x walked the same share
0.412 -> 0.091. The knob moves nitrogen between the medium and an intracellular store, and the
observable it was scored on counts BOTH sides, so it is blind to the transfer by construction.

**Why:** a null sweep is read as a property of the parameter, but it is a property of the
PAIR (parameter, observable). A flat response licenses "this knob is dead" only once you have
shown the observable can move at all under something.

**How to apply:** before concluding a knob is unreachable, ask what quantity the claim is made
against and whether your readout is the same quantity. Pair every flatness claim with an arm where
the SAME knob moves a related readout — [[feedback-a-non-vacuity-check-can-itself-be-vacuous]].
