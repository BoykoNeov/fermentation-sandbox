A source transcribed for one purpose usually carries more than that purpose used. Before
recording a quantity as unsourced, **grep your own repo's transcriptions for it**, not just the
corpus.

**Why:** D-209 transcribed Peyer 2017 Table 16 — a malt wort's 18 free amino acids — to derive
`nitrogen_uptake_charge_beer`, a charge *per mole of nitrogen*. Only the table's **ratios** were
needed, so only the ratios were used. The **total** of that same table is the wort's assimilable
nitrogen, which is exactly the quantity D-222 called unsourced ("Tyrell prints no FAN … so
nothing sources a repair"). That sentence rode a test docstring and the memory ledger for eight
records while the number sat in the repo, already units-cross-checked. At D-230 it summed to
164 mg N/L + 25-30 ammonium and closed the candidate in one afternoon.

**How to apply:** when a beat says "nothing sources X", ask what X is dimensionally (a total? a
ratio? a rate?) and search the repo's own transcribed tables and provenance notes for something
that *contains* it, even under another parameter's name. A derivation that used a table's shape
has probably left its magnitude unread. Related: [[feedback-re-read-the-source-you-already-mined]]
(same trap one level out — the source on disk), [[feedback-a-notes-field-is-unchecked-storage]],
[[feedback-check-the-blocker-is-still-blocking]].
