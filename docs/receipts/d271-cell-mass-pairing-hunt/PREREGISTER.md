# Pre-registration — the count-plus-weight pairing hunt (opened 2026-09-05)

## The target
D-219's open item, restated by D-232 and still unsupplied: **a source pairing a yeast CELL
COUNT with a GRAVIMETRIC DRY WEIGHT in one ferment at one timepoint.** It scores D-230
branch 1 (Tyrell's cells are heavier than the engine's gram), whose demand is **70.9-91.9 pg/cell** (D-230, UNCHANGED by D-270). D-270 sec 7 re-priced the
ENGINE-SIDE elemental estimate 34.87 -> 47.71-62.12 pg; that narrowed the GAP to 1.14-1.93x.
The 47.7-62.1 is the engine's estimate, NOT Tyrell's demand -- an error this file made on its
first draft and corrects here.

## What a hit does and does not do
The engine's 4e-11 g is **D-219's DEFINITION** of the gram Coleman's fit is counted in, not a
competing estimate. A literature pg/cell therefore **cannot move the engine's gram**; it can
only score branch 1.

## ADMISSIBILITY, fixed BEFORE looking
A glucose-limited aerobic chemostat cell is not an ale cell budding in wort, and D-267's own
record already warns about carrying Lange & Heijnen's composition to a fermenting must.
So, decided in advance:

* A chemostat cells-per-gram figure is admissible as a **BOUND on cell mass**, never as a
  value for ale-in-wort.
* Therefore it **moves branch 1 only if it lands OUTSIDE 47.7-62.1 pg**. A figure landing
  inside the demand is NOT a confirmation of branch 1 — it is consistent with it and with
  nothing else being true, and must be reported as such.
* The product-dosing frame (g of dry/compressed product per cell count) stays REJECTED
  (D-219, three instances: Handbook of Enology x2, Concepts in Wine Chemistry).

## Corpus result, established before the external search
Two-sided negative, four named instances:
* Crepin 2017 S1 — weighs dry biomass gravimetrically (filtered, 105 C / 48 h), counts NOTHING.
* Varela 2004 — counts (Neubauer) only the 1e6 cells/mL INOCULUM; results are dry weight only.
* Foster 2022 — counts cells (haemocytometer); dry weight appears ONLY as a trehalose
  normaliser, not as a fermentation timepoint.
* Tyrell 2013 — counts cells in suspension, no weight.
